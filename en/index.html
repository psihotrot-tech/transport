Services

Everything your car needs when it can't move on its own

We transport cars, vans and light vehicles locally, across Serbia and from abroad.

Towing & roadside assistance

Breakdown, flat battery, puncture or a car that won't start. We come to you and take the vehicle to a garage, your home or wherever you need.

Accident recovery

Damaged vehicles are loaded with care and taken to a garage, an assessor or secure parking.

Car transport from abroad

Bought a car in Germany, Austria or elsewhere in the EU? We collect it from the seller and deliver it to your door in Serbia.

Transport for dealers and garages

New and used cars, classic cars and unregistered vehicles, for companies and private customers.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked

How quickly can you get to me?

It depends on where you are and on traffic. Call us or send your location and you will get an arrival estimate straight away.

How much does towing cost?

The price depends on distance, vehicle type and its condition. You get a quote on request and know the price before we set off.

Do you work at night, at weekends and on holidays?

Yes. Our towing service runs 24/7, every day of the year.

Can you bring a car from Germany to Serbia?

Yes. We collect the vehicle from the seller abroad and deliver it to the address you choose. Send us the vehicle details and pick-up location for a quote.

What should I have ready when I contact you?

Your exact location, the make and model, whether the wheels turn and whether you have the key. A photo of the vehicle via WhatsApp or Viber helps.
